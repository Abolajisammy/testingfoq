Qigong (pronounced chee gong)

What is Qigong?

It's an ancient Chinese practice believed to promote longevity by nourishing your "Qi" (life force energy) and balancing your body and mind.Imagine a gentle blend of stretches, coordinated breathing, and mindful meditation. The movements are slow flowing postures coordinated with breath. Focused attention on visualizations calm the mind and guide the flow of Qi.

  • Reduces stress and anxiety
  • Increases energy
  • Improves sleep quality
  • Boosts immune function
  • Enhances balance and coordination
  • Promotes emotional well-being
  • Supports healthy aging

Qigong is accessible to everyone, regardless of age or fitness level. You can even practice it in a chair or wheelchair! So, if you're looking for a gentle yet powerful way to enhance your health and well-being, give qigong a try!
