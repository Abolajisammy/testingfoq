We can customize the retreat experience for you

Here are some of the options!

Breathwork
A guided practice using controlled breathing techniques to release stress, regulate emotions, and increase energy. You might feel tingling, emotional release, or deep relaxation.
Qigong
A gentle movement and breathing practice from Chinese medicine designed to cultivate life energy ("qi"). Expect slow, flowing motions, deep breathing, and a meditative, calming effect.
Massages
A hands-on body therapy that relieves muscle tension, improves circulation, and promotes relaxation. You’ll lie down while a therapist applies pressure to muscles, usually in a soothing environment.
Meditation
A quiet, inward-focused practice aimed at calming the mind, improving focus, and cultivating awareness. Expect stillness, silence, and possibly a sense of mental clarity or peace.
Hiking
A mindful walk through nature designed to ground your energy and connect you with the natural world. Each step encourages presence, reflection, and rejuvenation while strengthening the body and calming the mind.
