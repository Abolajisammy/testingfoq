Ecstatic Dance Journey

Ecstatic Dance: Move. Release. Awaken.

A free-flowing, embodied movement practice that blends expression, rhythm, and emotion into a meditative dance experience. Ecstatic Dance invites you to reconnect with your body’s natural rhythm, release stuck energy, and awaken joy from within.

By moving intuitively — without judgment or choreography — you stimulate circulation, balance your energy, and cultivate deep inner harmony. It’s more than dance; it’s a journey back to yourself.

    What’s Included in Your Session:

  • Gentle warm-up (such as breathwork)
  • Opening circle to ground and connect
  • 90-minute Ecstatic Dance experience
  • Integration period (such as meditation or deep rest)
  • Closing circle to share, reflect, and seal the space

    What to wear?

  • Wear comfy clothes that let you stretch, flow, and dance freely.
  • Bring a water bottle — staying hydrated keeps your energy vibrant.
  • Most importantly, come with an open heart, curious spirit, and a body ready to move like no one’s watching.

    • $270 per Session
    Up to 20 participants - Available for private groups or events

What to Expect

Expect to move, breathe, laugh, and reconnect with your essence in the most natural, freeing way. Each session is intentionally designed to help you unwind, express yourself fully, and feel alive in your body.

Ready to dance with intention and joy? Contact us to schedule your private or group session.

Chakradance

Chakradance is a soul-nourishing journey that invites you to reconnect with your true self through rhythm, guided visualization, and free-flowing movement. There are no steps to follow — just close your eyes, tune in, and let the rhythm of the chakras guide you.
This 60-minute experience blends movement, music, and meditation to gently unblock and balance your energy centers, helping you release what no longer serves and return to your natural flow.
Who It’s For:
  • No dance or yoga experience needed
  • Suitable for all fitness levels and ages 16+
  • Come as you are — all you need is an open heart and a curious spirit
    • Wear comfortable clothes you can move in, and bring water to stay hydrated.
  • 60-minute guided Chakradance journey
  • Ideal for private groups, retreats, or wellness events
  • $180 per session (up to 15 participants)

Why Chakradance?

Move your body. Shift your energy. Awaken what’s within, and reconnect with the essence of who you truly are.
