Ecstatic Dance

A free-flowing movement practice that blends expression, rhythm, and emotion into a meditative dance experience. Ecstatic Dancing helps you reconnect with your body’s natural rhythm, release tension, and awaken joy from within. By moving intuitively and without judgment, you stimulate circulation, balance your energy, and cultivate inner harmony — promoting both physical vitality and emotional well-being.

  • A warm-up activity (such as breathwork)
  • An opening circle
  • A 90 Minute Ecstatic Dance Experience
  • A wind-down period (such as Meditation)
  • A closing circle
  • Wear comfy clothes that let you stretch, flow, and dance without limits.
  • Don’t forget your water bottle and sip often; staying hydrated keeps your energy high.
  • Most importantly, bring your open heart, curious spirit, and a body ready to let loose and dance like nobody’s watching!

Chakradance

Step into a candle-lit space where movement becomes meditation and music becomes medicine. Chakradance is a soul-nourishing journey that helps you reconnect with your true self through rhythm, guided visualization, and creative expression. No steps to learn—just move freely, feel deeply, and let the music of your chakras lead the way.
Who It’s For:
No dance or yoga experience needed—just an open heart and a curious spirit. Suitable for all fitness levels and ages 16+. Wear comfy clothes and bring water.

60 minute experience to unblock the chakras through movement , music and meditation.

$180 for a group of up to 15 people
