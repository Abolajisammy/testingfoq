Meditation

Guide to Meditation

Meditation, in its simplest form, is the practice of focusing your attention and becoming aware of the present moment. It's about training your mind to be less reactive and more observant of your thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations without judgment. Practicing meditation daily helps improve overall wellbeing. Meditation is a skill that takes practice, but even a few minutes a day can make a difference. Remember, there's no "right" way to meditate, so be patient with yourself and find a technique that works best for you.
