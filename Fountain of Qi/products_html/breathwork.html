Breathwork

Breathwork Journey: Free Your Qi.

Breathwork has been practiced for thousands of years in various cultures and traditions. We use various breathing techniques and exercises that intentionally influence your mental, emotional, and physical state. It's not just about breathing deeply, but about harnessing your breath as a powerful tool for transformation and well-being. Unlike our usual unconscious breathing, breathwork involves bringing awareness to your breath and intentionally modifying its rhythm, depth, and patterns. Breathwork is a powerful tool.

    Breathwork Techniques
  • Beginner’s Breath for Relaxation: A guided four-sided breath practice to calm the nervous system. Great for when feeling stressed, agitated, or overwhelmed.
  • Box Breath: A continuous cycle of a 4-count inhale, 4-count hold, 4-count exhale, and 4-count hold to regulate the nervous system. This is a more advanced version of Beginner’s Breath.
  • Breath Journey: A guided process to help clear trauma, regulate the nervous system, improve sleep, create clarity, and bring awareness to your inner state. A typical session lasts 90 minutes to 2 hours and is available for both group and individual session.

A transformational experience to release tension, reduce stress, and deeply connect with your inner self.
