Meet Our Founder

Nicole Timmerman

Inspiring Well-being, One Breath, One Adventure at a Time

Since 1997, Nicole has worn many hats, all with the same dedication – guiding people towards radiant well-being. As a seasoned massage therapist, she kneads away tension, not just from muscles, but from minds and souls. In 2022, her passion blossomed into a thriving wellness business, a haven for those seeking harmony within.

Nicole's magic extends beyond the massage table. She orchestrates soul-stirring retreats, where mountains whisper wisdom, and nature paints tranquil backdrops for yoga poses that melt away stress. Her voice, gentle yet powerful, leads participants through the transformative landscapes of meditation, qigong, and breathwork, unlocking reservoirs of inner peace.

But Nicole's rhythm isn't confined to tranquil stillness. Music sets her soul ablaze, and her body pulses with the joy of movement. Hike with her, and you'll witness her infectious laughter echoing through the pines. Paddle alongside her down roaring rapids, and you'll see her eyes sparkle with the thrill of nature's untamed beauty. On sun-kissed beaches, she'll jump in the ocean with her two sons, their joyous yelps painting the perfect soundtrack to a life overflowing with love.

This is Nicole, authentic and connected. She finds humor in everyday moments, transforming life's little wrinkles into laughter lines. A wife, a mother, a teacher, a healer, and a seeker of joy – she invites you to join her on this beautiful, messy, magnificent journey called life.

Because with Nicole Timmerman, well-being isn't just a destination, it's a vibrant dance you do every single day.
